# Returns and Refunds

Live as the store page /pages/returns (linked in the footer). Paste into Shopify, Settings, Policies, Refund policy.

Every Elsewhere Supply Co. piece is printed just for you when you order it. Because of that, we can't take returns or exchanges for a change of mind or the wrong size, so please check the size chart on the product page before you order.

## If something is wrong with your order
If your item arrives damaged, misprinted, defective, or it's not what you ordered, we'll make it right. Email thomasdbiz26@gmail.com within 30 days of delivery with:
- your order number
- a photo of the problem (and of the label, for a wrong item)

We'll send a free replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer. You don't need to send the item back.

## Lost packages
If your tracking hasn't moved in 10 business days, or it says delivered and you can't find it, email us and we'll work with the carrier to sort it out. If the package is truly lost, we'll send a replacement.

## Wrong address
Please double check your shipping address at checkout. If an order comes back to us because the address was wrong or incomplete, we can reship it once you cover the new shipping cost.

## Cancellations
Orders go to print quickly. Email us within 12 hours of ordering and we'll cancel it with a full refund. After that, it's already being made.

## Refunds
Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.
