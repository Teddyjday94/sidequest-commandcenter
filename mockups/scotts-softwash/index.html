Why soft wash

Clean that lasts, without the damage.

High pressure moves dirt around and can tear up siding and shingles. Soft washing kills mold and mildew at the root, then rinses gently.

Regular pressure washing

  • Can strip paint and etch wood
  • Forces water behind siding
  • Mildew grows back in weeks
  • Not safe for most roofs

Scott's soft wash

  • Low pressure, safe on every surface
  • Kills growth at the root
  • Stays clean for months
  • Roof-safe, landscaping-safe
