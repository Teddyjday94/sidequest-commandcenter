Every mockup and client site is rendered from this kit. The agent writes only the facts and short copy for a business; the kit handles the layout, colors, fonts, icons and phone layout. Each build has to pass two quality checks before it can be deployed.

How a build runs

  1. SpecThe agent fills in the business's real facts from the lead row and case file: services, phone, towns, their own photos, reviews quoted word for word.
  2. RenderThe kit turns the spec into the page, or four pages for the Full package.
  3. Text checkBlocks em dashes, AI-sounding phrases, leftover placeholders, missing tap-to-call, broken links, images without alt text.
  4. Browser checkOpens the page at phone and laptop width: no sideways scrolling, no broken images, no clipped headings. The agent looks at the screenshots.
  5. Deploy and verifyFix and re-check until both pass, then deploy to Vercel and confirm the live link loads.
